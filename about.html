About Fashion Journal

Our story

Why a fashion journal?

Fashion can feel like a world with its own secret language. Fashion Journal was started to open that language up: to explain the terms, tell the stories behind familiar clothes and help people find a style that feels like their own.

We publish a plain-English glossary, a short history of modern fashion and simple tools such as a style quiz and a silhouette guide. Our writing draws on fashion and textile history references, and we aim to be accurate, inclusive and practical.

Fashion Journal is independent. We don’t sell clothes, accept payment for coverage or promote particular brands.
